Refund & Cancellation Policy

(Clear, Stripe-Compatible)

Effective Date: [07 - 01 - 2026]

1. Subscription Cancellation

  • You may cancel your subscription at any time

  • Services will remain active until the billing period ends

2. Refunds

  • INTAKORS does not offer refunds for partial billing periods

3. Failed or Duplicate Payments

Payment issues such as failed or duplicate charges are handled via Stripe. For billing concerns, please contact us at operations@intakors.com.
