INTAKORS does not directly collect, store, or process any personal data through this website.

All personal and payment information (such as name, email, billing details, and payment information) is:

3. How We Use Information

INTAKORS does not access or use customer payment data.

INTAKORS does not retain personal data.

5. Your Rights

Any rights relating to personal or payment data (access, correction, deletion) should be exercised directly with Stripe, as they are the data controller for payment information.
